# About me

I'm Abdullah, a final-year Computer Science student at the University of Management and Technology (UMT). My main interests are deep learning research and cybersecurity, and I also like building complete AI products from the database all the way up to the API.

On the research side, I'm drawn to security applications of machine learning: intrusion detection systems, phishing detection, adversarial machine learning, and federated learning. I've also explored multi-agent LLM security and attacks on the memory and planner components of LLM agents.

On the product side, I co-own Resona, an AI research platform. I handle the whole backend, the machine learning parts and the infrastructure, while my partner Sharina handles the frontend.

I also take freelance work on Fiverr, mostly text classification and email classification projects.

My long-term goal is to do deep learning research and eventually build a significant AI venture. I want to be the kind of person who can both read the papers and ship the system.
